# Preflop card for deep-stack cash games (built for about 200bb), mostly 4-5 players.
# It is wider than the earlier card and leans toward standard deep-stack charts: wider opens,
# more 3-bets and fewer flat calls against a single raise, and a wide big blind defence.
#
# Some 3-bets are semi-bluffs, not only value: suited aces such as A5s hold an ace the raiser
# needs and make the nut flush draw, and suited connectors make straights and flushes that win
# big pots when stacks are deep. Everything here is a rule of thumb, not a solved range:
# no public solver numbers exist for multi-limper, no-rake home games.
#
# Notation: 77+ is 77 through AA. ATs+ is ATs through AKs. J4s+ is J4s through JTs.
# AQo+ is AQo and AKo.

# Facing one raise and no callers. The reraise and call lines are for the cutoff and button.
# 3-bet big hands for value, and some suited aces and suited connectors as semi-bluffs; few hands
# just call. A lone raise usually comes from a wide late opener, so king-queen 3-bets for value.

# Early and middle seats raise and call far less: players are still to act behind them.
# Ace-queen offsuit folds here.

# From the small or big blind you play out of position, so fewer hands 3-bet as semi-bluffs.

note.seats = Count the players still to act behind you: 5 or more is early, 4 is middle, 3 is cutoff, 2 is button, 1 is small blind. At 5 players the first to act is middle. At 4 players the first to act is cutoff.
note.open = Folded to you: raise to 3bb with your seat's range, otherwise fold.
note.iso = One or more limpers: raise to 4bb plus 1bb per limper with iso.raise, and from the cutoff or button also with iso.late.raise. Limp along with the rest of iso.limp only from the cutoff, button or small blind, or when two or more have limped. Fold the rest.
note.face_raise = One raise and no callers: early and middle seats have the tightest lists, the cutoff and button 3-bet and call wider, and the blinds have their own lists. Some suited aces and suited connectors 3-bet as semi-bluffs. From the big blind, also call many more suited hands, every offsuit hand with two cards ten or higher, and ace-nine, ace-eight, jack-nine and ten-nine offsuit. 3-bet to about 3 times the raise, call or fold.
note.callers = A raise and at least one caller: use the callers chart. Fewer hands 3-bet here, and hands that make big hands multiway get better. 3-bet to 3 times the raise plus one more raise per caller. From the big blind, also call the other offsuit hands with two cards ten or higher.
note.three_bet = You opened and got 3-bet: use the in-position chart if you did not open the small blind and the 3-bettor is in a blind, otherwise the out-of-position chart. Both charts are the same: 4-bet kings or aces to about 2.5 times the 3-bet and fold everything else, queens and ace-king included.
note.four_bet = You 3-bet and got 4-bet: go all in with kings or aces and fold everything else, queens and ace-king included.
note.big_blind = In the big blind with no raise, check. With limpers, raise iso.raise hands and check the rest.
