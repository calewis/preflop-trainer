note.seats = Count the players still to act behind you: 5 or more is early, 4 is middle, 3 is cutoff, 2 is button, 1 is small blind. At 5 players the first to act is middle. At 4 players the first to act is cutoff.
note.open = Folded to you: raise to 3bb with your seat's range, otherwise fold.
note.iso = One or more limpers: raise to 4bb plus 1bb per limper with iso.raise, and from the cutoff or button also with iso.late.raise. Limp along with the rest of iso.limp only from the cutoff, button or small blind, or when two or more have limped. Fold the rest.
note.face_raise = One raise and no callers: 3-bet for value only, sized big against players who call too much, about 14bb in position and 16bb from the big blind over a 3bb opening raise. Early and middle seats play tightest, cutoff and button 3-bet a wider value range (including tens, nines and strong suited aces) while calling smaller pairs, suited connectors and weaker suited broadways in position, and the blinds 3-bet a tighter linear core while calling more suited hands from the big blind and folding weaker offsuit hands.
note.callers = A raise and at least one caller: use the callers chart. Fewer hands 3-bet here, and hands that make big hands multiway get better. 3-bet to 3 times the raise plus one more raise per caller. From the big blind, also call the other offsuit hands with two cards ten or higher.
note.three_bet = You opened and got 3-bet: use the in-position chart if you did not open the small blind and the 3-bettor is in a blind, otherwise the out-of-position chart. 4-bet only kings and aces to about 2.5 times the 3-bet for value, because these games call too much for bluff 4-bets to work. Deep at 200bb, call with pairs, ace-king, suited broadways, strong and wheel suited aces, and suited connectors; fold ace-queen offsuit, ace-jack offsuit, king-queen offsuit and weaker.
note.four_bet = You 3-bet and got 4-bet: go all in with aces, call with kings and fold the rest against a tight 4-bet. Calling rather than shoving kings keeps the pot smaller against the tightest 4-bettors. Against players whose only re-raise over a 3-bet is a shove with aces or kings, kings themselves are a fold (about 23% equity against that range).
note.big_blind = In the big blind with no raise, check. With limpers, raise iso.raise hands and check the rest.
