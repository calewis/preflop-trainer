Flop Play

Play a betting round on the flop, then the turn is dealt and the hand stops. You act before seeing any equity — ranges and numbers are locked until you commit — then you're graded on your equity against the live ranges, the pot odds, implied odds on your draws, and how often these opponents actually fold. Villains use simple heuristics; they're here to make the hand play out, not to model good postflop poker.
