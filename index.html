Who you are playing

A seat left on Random draws someone new each time you shuffle or start a session. Pick a name and they keep that seat, including through a shuffle. Each name always plays its own settings — working out what those are is the exercise.

Starting stacks

Where you sit

Position is most of preflop and the ranges you are marked against are per-position, so Rotate moves you one seat every hand and deals you the whole table over time. Pick a position to drill it. Applies to both Play and Train.

Preflop ranges

One file does both jobs: the opponents open, 3-bet and defend from it, and trainer mode marks you against the same rows. Load your own to drill a different set of spots.
